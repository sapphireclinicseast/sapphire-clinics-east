HR08 Admin
Admin/Front Desk staff assess all clinical staff + each other in the same branch.
HR08 Peer
Clinical staff assess same-dept peers who share at least one work day. Min. 2 assessors per therapist.
HR09 Monthly
All clinical staff assess one admin/front desk staff member per month. Rotates randomly — full coverage by year end.
HR08 – Admin assesses All (Full-Year) All admin and front desk staff in the selected branch will be assigned to assess: every clinical staff member + all other admin staff. Period month is set to 0 (full-year). Existing assignments for the same year are skipped.
HR08 – Peer assesses Peer (Full-Year) For each clinical staff member, this will find peers in the same department who share at least one work day (from Decking config). It then picks 2–3 peers at random to be the assessors. Period month = 0 (full-year). Existing pairs are skipped.
HR09 – Clinical assesses Admin (Monthly Rotation) Each clinical staff member is assigned one admin/front desk staff to assess for the selected month. The rotation order is randomized per year using a deterministic seed so all admin staff are covered evenly by year end.
