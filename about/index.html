Who We Are

About Sapphire Clinics East, Inc.

Sapphire Clinics East, Inc. (SCEI) is dedicated to optimizing functional mobility, effective communication, and inclusive care — backed by evidence-based rehabilitation. SCEI operates branches under the Aura Health Clinic brand.

Our Mission
To optimize quality of life through accessible, excellent, and evidence-based rehabilitation services — optimizing functional mobility, enabling effective communication, promoting inclusivity, advancing clinical research, and fostering excellence in the professional community.
Our Vision
To lead rehabilitation in the Philippines. A global leader in accessible, excellent, and evidence-based habilitation and rehabilitation services — helping improve the overall health of society, one person and one community at a time.

Feeling seen.  Finding footing.  Moving forward.

🚀 Our BHAG — Big Hairy Audacious Goal
To be the #1 market leader in the rehabilitation space in the Philippines in 10 years.
