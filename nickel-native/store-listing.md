## Full description
Nickel brings licensed physical therapists into Filipino homes. Book a session in a few taps, see the rate before you confirm, and recover in the room you actually live in — no traffic, no waiting room, no guessing what it will cost.

Every therapist on Nickel is licensed and verified. Rates are set by the therapist and shown upfront, with transport included where stated.

WHY NICKEL
• Care at home, on your schedule — a licensed therapist comes to you.
• Verified professionals — every therapist is PRC-licensed and identity-verified.
• Easy, cashless payment — pay securely by card, GCash, Maya and more.
• You choose your professional — compare by rating, experience and rate.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Tell us your city and browse verified therapists near you.
2. Pick a therapist and a time that works.
3. Confirm and pay securely — they come to your home.

NEED A REFERRAL?
Home physical therapy in the Philippines requires a doctor’s referral. Don’t have one? Book a rehab-medicine consult inside Nickel — by video or in person — and get your referral.

FOR THERAPISTS
Set your own rate and schedule, get matched with clients near you, and get paid weekly. Nickel keeps a flat ₱20 per session — you keep the rest, net of payment fees.

Nickel is operated by Sapphire Clinics East, Inc.
