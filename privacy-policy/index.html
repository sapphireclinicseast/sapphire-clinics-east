Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sapphire Clinics East, Inc. (“SCEI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operating the Aura Health Rehab clinics in Pasig (East) and San Juan / Greenhills, is committed to protecting the privacy of our patients, their families, our staff, and the visitors of this website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over your information.

We process personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Sapphire Clinics East, Inc. is a registered company compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

This policy covers both our clinical services and this website. By using our services or this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood how your information is handled as described here.

1.Information we collect

Depending on your relationship with us, we may collect the following categories of information:

Personal information

  • Full name, date of birth, sex, civil status, nationality, and government-issued identifiers where required;
  • Contact details such as address, mobile and landline numbers, and email address;
  • Emergency contact and, for minors or dependents, the details of a parent, guardian, or authorized representative.

Sensitive personal information

  • Health and medical information — including referrals, diagnoses, assessments, therapy and treatment records, session notes, and progress reports;
  • Information necessary for care and billing, such as HMO or insurance details and, where applicable, PWD or senior citizen status;
  • Other sensitive information you choose to share that is relevant to your care.

Information collected through this website

  • Details you submit through our online forms, registration pages, or booking tools;
  • Messages you send us through email, our contact channels, or our Facebook and Viber accounts;
  • Technical and usage data collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 4).

2.How we use your information

We use your information only for legitimate purposes related to your care and to the operation of our clinics, including to:

  • Provide, coordinate, and document rehabilitation services — Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Speech-Language Pathology, Special Education (SPED), and Psychological Services;
  • Schedule appointments and send reminders, results, and care-related notifications;
  • Process billing, payments, HMO or insurance claims, and reward-program membership;
  • Respond to your inquiries, requests, and concerns;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, accreditation, and professional obligations;
  • Improve our services and, where appropriate, conduct research or training using information that has been anonymized or de-identified.

3.How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when necessary and with appropriate safeguards, including with:

  • Care providers within our clinics who are directly involved in your treatment;
  • HMOs, insurers, and payment processors, where you are availing of covered services or making payments;
  • Service providers and partners (for example, IT, communications, or laboratory partners) who process information on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection agreements;
  • Government agencies, regulators, or courts, where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where we engage third parties to process personal information on our behalf, we require them to protect it consistently with this policy and the Data Privacy Act.

4.Website, cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working properly, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies through the consent banner, and you can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Some pages link to services operated by our partners (for example, our online store and appointment tools). When you follow those links, the privacy practices of those services will also apply.

5.How we protect your information

We implement organizational, physical, and technical measures to safeguard your information against loss, unauthorized access, or misuse, including:

  • Limiting access to personal and health information to authorized personnel who need it to perform their duties;
  • Confidentiality obligations for our staff and partners;
  • Secure storage of records, and secure disposal of physical records and permanent deletion of digital files when they are no longer needed;
  • Reasonable safeguards for the systems we use to store and transmit information.

While we take these precautions seriously, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We continuously review our safeguards to keep them appropriate to the information we hold.

6.Data retention

We keep personal and health information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal, regulatory, and professional record-keeping requirements. When information is no longer required, we securely dispose of or permanently delete it.

7.Your rights as a data subject

Under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights over your personal information:

Right to be informedTo know whether and how your personal data is being processed.
Right to accessTo obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to rectificationTo correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
Right to erasure or blockingTo have your data removed or blocked under the conditions allowed by law.
Right to objectTo object to certain processing of your personal data.
Right to data portabilityTo obtain and reuse your data in a portable format, where applicable.
Right to file a complaintTo lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
Right to damagesTo be indemnified for damages due to inaccurate, unlawful, or unauthorized processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

8.Minors and dependents

Many of our patients are children or dependents. Where a patient is a minor or otherwise unable to provide consent, we collect and process information with the consent and involvement of a parent, guardian, or authorized representative, and only for purposes related to the patient's care.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and post the current version on this page.
