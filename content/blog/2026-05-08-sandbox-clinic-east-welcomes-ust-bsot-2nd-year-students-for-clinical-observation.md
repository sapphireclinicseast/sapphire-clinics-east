## A Genuine Glimpse Into Clinical Practice

Beyond passive observation, the students were given meaningful opportunities to participate in the therapeutic process. Under the close guidance of our senior clinicians, they were able to:

* **Interview parents and guardians,** learning how to gather developmental histories, identify family priorities, and build the kind of rapport that anchors every successful intervention.
* **Perform screening tests,** gaining first-hand exposure to the clinical reasoning behind assessment selection, administration, and interpretation.
* **Conduct treatment activities** with clients, putting their growing skills into practice in a safe, supervised environment.

Every interaction was carried out under the strict supervision of our occupational therapists, ensuring that learning never came at the expense of client safety, dignity, or quality of care.

## Building the Future of Occupational Therapy, Together

Hosting students from one of the country's premier occupational therapy programs is more than a tradition for us — it is a privilege and a responsibility. Each batch of learners reminds us why we do what we do, and pushes us to keep refining our practice as both clinicians and educators.

To the UST BSOT 2nd year students: thank you for bringing your energy, your questions, and your hearts into our clinic. We hope these four days have planted seeds that will grow into long, meaningful careers of service.

We look forward to welcoming you back — and to walking alongside you as you continue your journey toward becoming the occupational therapists our communities need.
