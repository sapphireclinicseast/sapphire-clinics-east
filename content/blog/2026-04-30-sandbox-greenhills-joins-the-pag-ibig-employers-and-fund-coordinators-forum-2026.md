# Sandbox Greenhills Joins the PAG-IBIG Employers and Fund Coordinators Forum 2026

*Published April 30, 2026*

## A Welcome From the PAG-IBIG San Juan Team

Our team was warmly received by the dedicated officers of PAG-IBIG San Juan, whose insights and guidance set the tone for a productive and engaging session:

* **Melissa Cammayo** – Branch Head, PAG-IBIG San Juan
* **John Andrew Eripol** – Marketing Officer
* **Joyce Marie Valero** – Marketing Services IV

We extend our sincere thanks to Ms. Cammayo and her team for organizing a forum that was both informative and refreshingly practical. Their commitment to keeping employer-partners well-informed reflects PAG-IBIG's broader mission of empowering Filipino workers through accessible savings, housing, and short-term loan programs.

## Sandbox Greenhills in Attendance

Representing Sandbox Greenhills at the forum were:

* **John Paul De Los Santos**, Senior HR Officer
* **Jenevie Solar**, Senior Accounting Officer

Their participation reinforces our commitment to staying current with PAG-IBIG policies, contribution guidelines, and member benefits — ensuring that our employees continue to receive accurate, timely, and well-supported assistance whenever they engage with the Fund.

## Why This Matters to Our People

For Sandbox Greenhills, attending forums like these is more than a compliance exercise. It is part of how we care for our team. Every update we bring back — whether about contribution remittance, housing loan eligibility, or member savings — translates into smoother processes, clearer guidance, and better-informed employees who can confidently take advantage of the benefits they are entitled to.

We believe that a well-supported team is a thriving team, and partnerships with institutions like PAG-IBIG are central to that promise.

## Looking Ahead

Sandbox Greenhills remains committed to active collaboration with PAG-IBIG and other government agencies that protect and uplift the Filipino workforce. We look forward to applying the insights gained from this year's forum into even stronger HR and accounting practices in the months ahead.

To the PAG-IBIG San Juan team — thank you for your hospitality, your dedication, and your continued service. We are honored to be your partner in caring for our people.
